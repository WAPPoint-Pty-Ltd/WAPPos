# WAPPos + SoftPOS Setup Guide

Welcome to **WAPPos + SoftPOS by WAPPoint!**

To get started, you simply need to install two apps on your compatible Android phone or tablet.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

* An Android phone or tablet compatible with contactless payments.
* The **WAPPos APK** provided by WAPPoint.
* The **Halo Dot** app installed from the Google Play Store.
* Your WAPPos login credentials, provided by the WAPPoint Support team.
* An NFC-enabled device to accept contactless card payments.

## Step 1 – Install WAPPos

1. Locate the WAPPos APK file.
2. Download the APK file to your Android device.
3. Open the downloaded file and follow the on-screen instructions to install the app.
4. If Android displays a security warning, it may be because the app is being installed from outside the Google Play Store. Only proceed if you received the APK directly from WAPPoint and trust the source.

> **Important:** Only install APK files supplied by WAPPoint.

## Step 2 – Install Halo Dot

1. Open the Google Play Store on your Android device.
2. Search for **Halo Dot**.
3. Download and install the app.

You do not need to log in to Halo Dot or configure any settings.

Halo Dot runs in the background and handles the card payment functionality for your WAPPos system.

## Step 3 – Log in to WAPPos

1. Open the WAPPos App.
2. Log in using the credentials provided by the WAPPoint Support team.
3. Confirm that you can access your point-of-sale system.

If you cannot log in, contact WAPPoint Support for assistance.

## Step 4 – Test a Card Payment

Once both apps are installed and you have logged in to WAPPos, perform a test transaction.

1. Open the **Quick Sale** screen in WAPPos.
2. Create a sale for **R1.00**.
3. Select the card payment option.
4. When prompted, tap your own contactless bank card against the NFC area on the back of your Android device.
5. Follow the on-screen prompts to complete the transaction.
6. Confirm that the payment was successful.

If the transaction completes successfully, your WAPPos and SoftPOS setup is working.

> **Note:** Use your own card for testing and ensure the transaction is handled according to WAPPoint's test transaction and refund procedures.

## How It Works

| App          | Purpose                                                                  |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **WAPPos**   | Your point-of-sale system for managing products, sales and transactions. |
| **Halo Dot** | Handles card payment functionality in the background.                    |

You do not need to configure Halo Dot separately. Simply keep it installed on your device. You also don't need a login for it.

## Troubleshooting

### WAPPos will not install

* Confirm that the APK download completed successfully.
* Ensure your device is compatible with the supplied application.
* Check Android's installation permissions.
* Contact WAPPoint Support if the issue persists.

### Halo Dot does not appear to work

* Confirm that Halo Dot is installed.
* Ensure the app has not been disabled or removed.
* Restart your device and try the payment again.
* Do not attempt to configure or register Halo Dot independently.

### The card payment fails

* Confirm that NFC is enabled on your Android device, if required.
* Make sure you are tapping a contactless card against the device's NFC area.
* Check that both WAPPos and Halo Dot are installed.
* Retry the transaction and follow any prompts displayed on the screen.

If you continue experiencing issues, contact WAPPoint Support.

## Need Help?

If you experience any problems installing the apps, logging in, or processing a payment, please contact **WAPPoint Support**.

Your Android phone or tablet is now ready to function as your point-of-sale and contactless payment device.
